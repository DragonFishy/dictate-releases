# Dictate

Dictation for your Mac. Hold **fn**, speak, let go: your words come out right, in any app.
Everything runs on your Mac.

**Download:** the latest `.dmg` from [Releases](../../releases/latest).

## Installing

1. Open the DMG and drag **Dictate** into **Applications**.
2. The first time you open it, macOS says it can't check the app. Open **System Settings →
   Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to Dictate. You only do
   this once; updates install themselves after that.

Needs a Mac with Apple silicon. The AI features need macOS 26 or later with Apple
Intelligence turned on.

## Feedback

In the app: **Settings → Send feedback**.
